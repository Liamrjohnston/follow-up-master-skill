# Worked example: a wedding photographer

**The brief (made up for this example).**

> Lead went cold, help. I'm a wedding photographer in Bristol, packages £1,800 to £2,600.
>
> Jun 3, Hannah: Hi! Getting married 14 Sept next year at Ashton Court. Do you have availability and what are your prices?
> Jun 3, me: Congrats! I'm free that date. Packages attached, most couples go for Full Day at £2,400.
> Jun 4, Hannah: Love your work!! Do you do a shorter one? It's quite a small wedding, about 40 people, and my mum is helping pay.
> Jun 4, me: The Half Day is £1,800, 6 hours.
> Jun 5, Hannah: ok thanks, will chat to mum and Josh x
> Jun 12, me: Hi Hannah, just checking in!
> (nothing since, today is Jun 26)
>
> Facts: I can do a 4 hour package for £1,350 for small weddings, I just don't advertise it. I'd want a £300 deposit to hold the date.

**Reading it (silently).**
- Last real advance: none. She never agreed to a call or a hold.
- The turn: Jun 4, "Do you do a shorter one? ... my mum is helping pay." The answer was the cheapest listed package, still £1,800. Then "ok thanks".
- Who decides: "will chat to mum and Josh". Mum is helping pay.
- Left out: a £1,350 four hour package.
- Chasers: one generic, unanswered. So the plan allows message 1, message 2, then the last message.

Most likely: price, with a second decider (mum) who pays. Runner-up: never committed.

**The card.**

**Why they went quiet.**
Most likely **price, with mum as a second decision maker**. Hannah asked for something shorter for "quite a small wedding" and said "my mum is helping pay". The cheapest option she heard was £1,800, then "ok thanks". Runner-up: she never committed to anything, so this lead is cooler than it felt.

**Send this today.**
> Hi Hannah, I should have said this sooner: for small weddings like yours I do a 4 hour package at £1,350, which covers the ceremony, group shots and the first part of the reception. Happy to send a one page summary you can show your mum and Josh. Would it be a bad idea to send that over?

Why: it answers the money worry with a real option and gives her something to show the people who are paying.

**If no reply.**
In 5 to 7 days: "Hi Hannah, one thing that might help: a £300 deposit holds 14 Sept, and the rest isn't due until nearer the day. No rush either way."
A week after that: "Have you given up on wedding photos for September? Totally fine if so, I'll leave it with you."

**When to stop.**
After the last message, stop, whatever happens. If she says she's booked someone else, thank her and leave it there.

**Next time.**
When a couple says "small wedding" or mentions a parent paying, offer the 4 hour package in the first reply.

**Check these.**
- What the 4 hour package actually covers.
- That the deposit terms are as stated.

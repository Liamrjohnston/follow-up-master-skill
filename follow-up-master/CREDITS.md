# Credits

Follow-Up Master learned from these, in its own words. No text is copied.

- **Chris Voss**, *Never Split the Difference*, plus his Talks at Google talk and Black Swan Group videos on ghosted deals: labels, the accusation audit, no-oriented questions, and the "Have you given up on...?" question.
- **Matthew Dixon and Ted McKenna**, *The JOLT Effect*, from an author interview: indecision versus status quo, recommending instead of adding options, taking risk off the table.
- **Neil Rackham**, *SPIN Selling*, from an INSEAD interview and secondary summaries: advances versus continuations, implication and need-payoff questions.
- **Rob Fitzpatrick**, *The Mom Test*, from his own talk and reviews: compliments are not commitments.
- **Alex Hormozi**, free courses on his own channel: helping the contact sell it to the real decision maker.
- **Close.com**, the free Follow-Up Formula guide: set the stop before you start; don't switch channels to get around silence.

None of the numbers in these sources are used as claims by this skill.
